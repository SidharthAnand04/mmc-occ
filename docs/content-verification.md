## Confirm with MMC before production

1. **Plymouth telephone:** patient-forms body lists 651-398-8274 while its footer and the main contact page list 651-340-1445. This build consistently uses the contact-page main number.
2. **Collection windows:** the drug-testing page says appointments are not required; the footer limits screening walk-ins to before 3 pm; the contact page specifies 9 am–3 pm EScreen collection at Eagan and Maplewood. Copy preserves the cutoff, distinguishes EScreen, and asks callers to confirm service-specific availability. Verify Plymouth collection hours and current Saturday arrangements in Eagan.
3. **On-site testing:** MMC's homepage advertises 24x7 testing, but there is no clearly verified dedicated dispatch number, coverage radius, lead time, or after-hours process. The redesign describes the service without promising around-the-clock dispatch. Confirm operational details.
4. **MOH transition:** confirm any approved wording about the business relationship. No acquisition percentages, inherited provider roster, automatic records transfer, existing contract continuity, or MOH locations have been asserted. This page follows MMC's published invitation to establish services.
5. **Employer onboarding:** confirm authorization forms, billing setup, service pricing, secure intake destination, and response expectations. Until then, direct call/email contact is used. The website does not collect or store health information.
6. **Clinician roster:** names/credentials are taken from MMC's homepage. Confirm current availability by clinic. No additional credentials were inferred for clinicians with sparse profiles. No MOH clinician biographies were reused.
7. **Imagery:** workforce.webp is a resized WebP derivative of user-supplied `mmc_sample_site.xz`, path `mirrored_site/www.mohonline.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/iStock-1171238260_African-American-woman-working-at-construction-site-smiling.jpg`. It is illustrative stock photography, not an MMC employee/patient photograph. Confirm MMC's reuse license before production, or replace with commissioned/licensed MMC imagery. No MOH logos, staff photos, testimonials, or clinic photographs are used.
8. **Forms, payment and privacy:** links use existing MMC resources rather than copying medical forms or inventing policies. Confirm destination ownership, latest approved form versions, and functioning payment/booking flows before production. Test submissions/payments were not made.
9. **Brand:** typographic MMC identity and blue palette are a proposed redesign. Approve against the clinic's brand assets before launch.
